# Hi, I'm Firdaus Nuur Rhamadhan — Rhamaa

### Full-stack engineer building technology from devices to applications

I'm interested in the point where software meets the physical world. My work spans IoT and embedded systems, full-stack software, and early AI and computer-vision prototypes. I enjoy connecting devices, data, and applications into tools people can actually use.

Based in Bandung, Indonesia.

## What I work on

- **IoT and embedded systems** — connecting microcontrollers, sensors, firmware, and apps.
- **Software products** — building full-stack experiences and desktop tools, including [Captr Studio](https://github.com/rhamaa/Captr-Studio).
- **AI and computer vision** — exploring RAG/LLM assistants and practical computer-vision workflows.

My experience includes IoT engineering work with Kirei, BAPPEDA Jabar, Makerindo, and PPTIK ITB. I studied Informatics at Universitas Kebangsaan Republik Indonesia and Electronics at SMKN 1 Cimahi.
